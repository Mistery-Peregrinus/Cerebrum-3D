**CEREBRUM**
-English
Interactive 3D Atlas of the Brain and Cellular Neuroscience.

ABOUT THE PROJECT:
Greetings to all users. My name is Maximiliano Morales Domínguez, and this project is a 3D atlas of the anatomy of the entire brain, which also includes basic content on cellular neuroscience.
The purpose of this software is to provide free, high-quality knowledge to as many people as possible, especially students and professionals in the biological and health sciences.
Cerebrum has room to grow, so we welcome constructive feedback to add, remove, improve, or change any content or functionality.
Contact email: as.1759616@gmail.com

INSTRUCTIONS:
index.html contains the original version of the software. To view a preview, click on Dark.png
clear.html contains an alternative version with a different design (the features remain the same). To view a preview, click on Clear.png

WHAT IS INCLUDED:
- 3D brain: telencephalon, diencephalon, cerebellum, and brainstem, with lobes, internal structures, and over 100 fact sheets covering function, anatomy, blood supply, disorders, and what happens when they fail.
- Cellular neuroscience: morphological classification of neurons, real-time simulated action potential (Hodgkin-Huxley model), chemical and electrical synapses, and 9 neurotransmitters with their effects.
- Area search tool, labels on the model, and versions in Spanish and English.

SOURCES AND DISCLAIMER:
The information comes from highly reliable medical sources (NCBI StatPearls, MSD Manual, MedlinePlus, PubMed, WHO, and reference textbooks), selected by the author and cited in each fact sheet.
Educational content: This is not a substitute for evaluation by a healthcare professional.

Developed by Maximiliano Morales Domínguez, the project’s creator. Artificial intelligence tools (Claude Opus 5.5, by Anthropic) were used in the development, particularly for programming, 3D modeling, and information gathering.
